# Colour and emotion map

How colour is assigned in the assessment. Written down because the assignments are
arguable, and an arguable decision that lives only in CSS becomes folklore.

**The principle.** Colour follows the emotional job of the copy it sits behind, not a
fixed scheme applied evenly. A page can hold several pairings if each one is doing
work. The palette is fixed by v2.0.1; what varies is which colour meets which moment.

**Who this is for.** Residents and community leaders, not procurement. Provincial and
municipal buyers approve the product, but they do not use it. The way to win them is a
tool that residents actually finish, so every colour decision below is judged on how it
lands for a volunteer at their kitchen table, not on how it looks in a procurement deck.

---

## The emotional vocabulary

| Colour | What it does here | Where it belongs |
|---|---|---|
| **Gold** | Warmth, invitation, energy. The hero colour and the only call-to-action colour. | Openings, actions, the moment something is offered |
| **Deep Azure** | Calm, steadiness, being taken seriously. | Reassurance, structure, moments that must not feel alarming |
| **Forest Green** | Growth, work, the outdoors. Serious rather than soft. | Effort, progress, the parts that ask something of the reader |
| **Fresh Green** | Lightness, emergence, relief. | Encouragement, small beginnings, good news that is not a trophy |
| **Bold Red** | Alert only. §2.3 reserves it for warnings, and it stays reserved. | Validation errors. Nothing else. |
| **Charcoal / Serene White** | The ground everything else sits on. | Body copy everywhere |

---

## The five questions

Each factor takes the colour of what it asks about, so moving through the assessment
feels like moving rather than filling a form. All are Charcoal text on a tint, safe at
any size.

| Factor | Ground | Ratio | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hope and optimism | Gold 40% | 11.70:1 | The question is about belief. Warmth, not judgement. |
| Collaboration | Fresh Green | 13.34:1 | About people together. The lightest, most social ground. |
| Leadership | Deep Azure 20% | 10.35:1 | About who steps up. Steady without weight. Azure 40% was tried and sat heavy in the middle of the flow, which is where a volunteer is most likely to stop. |
| Progress | Forest Green 40% | 8.10:1 | About work that has actually happened. |
| Resilience | Gold 20% | 12.86:1 | About staying power. Warm rather than cool, and it bookends the Gold of question one: belief at the start, endurance at the end. |

## The five results

**This is the decision most likely to go wrong.** Colouring stages by how good they are
rebuilds the ranking the framework rejects: green for Growth and something cold for the
Waiting Place would tell the reader they failed, in a language they read before any
words.

So the assignment is by **what the reader needs on arriving there**, which comes out
deliberately non-monotonic. The sequence runs calm, warm, light, serious, calm again.
No hue is a score.

| Stage | Ground | Ratio | The reader's need |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waiting Place | Deep Azure 20% | 10.35:1 | Calm and permission. This is the hardest result to receive, so it gets the least alarming colour in the palette. |
| Impasse | Gold 40% | 11.70:1 | Recognition and warmth. The energy is real and stuck; the colour says so. |
| Catalytic | Fresh Green | 13.34:1 | Encouragement. Something is emerging and deserves to feel light. |
| Growth | Forest Green 60% | 5.87:1 | A challenge, not a trophy. The 40% stop was tried and read as drab rather than serious, which is the wrong note for the one result that should feel like responsibility. The 60% stop has enough saturation to feel deliberate. |
| Sustain & Renew | Deep Azure 40% | 7.33:1 | Steadiness and vigilance. The risk here is comfort, so it returns to calm rather than escalating. |

Note that Growth is **deeper** than Catalytic rather than brighter. That is intentional:
Harwood's central finding is that people assume they are already at Growth, so it is the
one result that should feel like responsibility rather than reward.

---

## What stays neutral

- **Body copy** is Charcoal on Serene White everywhere. Colour carries emotion; it never
  carries the reading.
- **The reflection line** sits on the plain ground with a rule, so it does not compete
  with the stage colour beside it.
- **The email gate** is deliberately the quietest block on the page. An ask should not
  shout.
- **Errors** are Bold Red, and Bold Red is nothing else.

---

## The rule that keeps this honest

Every ground above takes Charcoal text at any size. The six large-text pairings, Serene
White or Fresh Green on the three dark colours, are available for display type at 24px
regular or 18.7px bold and above, and are used only there.

If a colour cannot carry the text it needs to carry, the colour is wrong, not the text.
